# How to Cancel a Subscription

If a subscriber wants to cancel their subscription, you can do this by go to **WP-admin > Cart > Subscription**:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/subscription-screen-5f24601701.webp)

Click on their subscription, it should show their subscription details page:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/subscriptions-details-b3d4b1bdba.webp)

In the bottom left, click the “Cancel Subscription” button, it will show the cancel modal:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/cancel-subscription-4a3957fc61.webp)

In this modal, you could choose whether you want to cancel the subscription **immediately** or **end of current billing period**.

You could also have a choice whether you want to send a **refund** or **no refund**.
