# How to Move from Studiocart to PublishPress Cart

Table of Contents

- [How to Move from Studiocart](#0-toc-title)
- [Reconnecting Stripe](#1-toc-title)

The plugin Studiocart closed in 2026.

The good news is that the PublishPress team is providing a path forward for Studiocart users.

[PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) is a new plugin. It’s an improved and redesigned version of the Studiocart plugin.

For existing Studiocart users, there is a migration path to [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart). Moving to PublishPress Cart means that your site will be supported by a highly skilled development team with multiple developers who actively maintain and improve their products.

Here is a guide to migrating your site from Studiocart to PublishPress Cart.

## How to Move from Studiocart [#](#0-toc-title)

First let’s get the plugins installed for your migration. Log in to your website that has the Studiocart plugin.

- Deactivate the Studiocart plugin.
- Install the PublishPress Cart plugin.
- [Click here to download the PublishPress Cart migration plugin](https://app.box.com/s/j36zos5leuq4741rmvdo8d18z6a6m0mj).
- Install the PublishPress Cart migration plugin.

Your “Plugins” screen will now look this screenshot below. Only the “PublishPress Cart Migrations” and “PublishPress Cart Pro” plugins are active.

![Plugins ready to migrate](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/plugins-migration-start-0b27e7e7c9.webp)

- Click “Start your migration”.

![Start migration](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/start-migration-6557b74cd6.webp)

The next screen has the main migration process. **Please back up your site before continuing**.

- Click “Start your migration”.

![start my migration](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/start-migration-process-a24239c925.webp)

- Click the “Yes, start my migration” button twice:

![start my migration](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/yes-start-migration-1f131c5be0.webp)

This will complete the migration and you’ll see the message “Congratulations. Your migration is complete”. Welcome to PublishPress Cart!

You can now go to the “Plugins” screen and remove the PublishPress Cart Migrations plugin.

![Welcome to PublishPress Cart! ](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/migration-finished-88b42cb902.webp)

## Reconnecting Stripe [#](#1-toc-title)

If you’re using Stripe as your payment gateway, please reconnect your account.

- After the migration, please reconnect Stripe under Cart > Settings > Payment methods.

![Cart payment with Stripe](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/cart-payment-methods-50cacaefea.webp)

- Click “Connect with Stripe” to connect your account.

![Connect with Stripe](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/connect-with-stripe-910eb342a9.webp)

- You will now see a new screen hosted on Stripe.com where you can connect your account:

![Connect your Stripe account](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/new-connect-stripe-401b3352a4.webp)
