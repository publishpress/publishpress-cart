# Google ReCAPTCHA

Google ReCaptcha is available to add extra security to your checkout page. You can integrate Google ReCAPTCHA with [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) by adding your Google ReCAPTCHA Site and Secret Key in the Integrations settings tab.

**How To Integrate Google ReCAPTCHA With [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart)**

Visit the [Google ReCAPTCHA website](https://www.google.com/recaptcha/about/) to create a Site and Secret Key for your website. Select **V3 Admin Console** in the top right menu bar. Then select the **+** create button on the right.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/create-reacaptcha-e1e7ea572d.webp)

From here you can register your website, choose which type of verification you want to use and generate your Site and Secret key.

In the window copy your Google Recaptcha Site Key. Then head over to your WordPress Dashboard, select PublishPress Cart > Settings.  
Select the Integrations tab and paste the Google ReCAPTCHA Site Key into the matching field box. Repeat this process to copy and paste the Google ReCAPTCHA Secret Key.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/recaptcha-setting-db861c23b7.webp)

Scroll to the bottom of the Integrations window and select Save Settings. Google ReCAPTCHA is now linked to PublishPress Cart and will automatically appear on every checkout form.

![](https://studiocart.co/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/save-settings-1-1024x137.png)
