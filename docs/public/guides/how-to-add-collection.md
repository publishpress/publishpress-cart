# How to Add Collection

In [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart), we have a feature called “Collections”. Collection are multiple products combined into 1, and users can purchase that 1 collection product and they will get multiple products that you added there.

Here are steps to add a collection:

- Go to **WP-admin > Cart > Collections**:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/add-new-collection-742b61dcb1.webp)

- Click “**Add New Collection**“:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/new-collection-0b9a8f856e.webp)

- On this screen, you get the sam exact configuration when you create [new product](https://publishpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/creating-product.png), the only differences are instead of “Payment Plan”, you got the “Products & Pricing”. In this tab, you can choose between using **custom pricing** or use **product pricing**:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/products-and-pricing-d60b9e1e4a.webp)

- If you choose **custom prices**, you will have the option to set the payment plan:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/payment-plan-collection-5db8d56f17.webp)

- This payment plan will be overwriting the actual product pricing. You can set the pricing to be one time, or recurring as a subscription.
- After you setup the pricing, make sure to input your product in the add product section:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/add-product-collection-d3e32030f4.webp)

- If you choose product prices, you will only ended up with add product section, with the payment plan selection that pulling from the product:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/payment-plan-product-prices-030d816a21.webp)

- You can add another product by click the “Add Product” in the bottom right:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/add-new-product-collection-338da8b58c.webp)

- After you click the button, it should show another product added at the bottom of first product:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/multiple-product-collections-c248e20314.webp)

- After you done configuring your collection, you could save and publish it.
- This is how it looks on the frontend after published:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/collection-frontend-e50ccc3aec.webp)
