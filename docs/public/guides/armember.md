# ARMember

Do you use ARMember? The ARMember integration allows you to automatically activate, deactivate or terminate new users to your membership plans. You can integrate your account with [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) by installing the plugin on your WordPress site.

**How To Integrate ARMember With [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart)**

Get started by Installing and Activating the ARMember Plugin on your WordPress site. Then configure the settings in the ARMember plugin.

Now you can set up a ARMember integration by going to a PublishPress Cart product and selecting “ARMember” from the services menu in the Integrations tab. From here you’ll see a list of the membership plans you previously created in ARMember. Choose the plan you would like to link to this product.

In the example below the following will occur from the chosen trigger and payment settings. When a product is purchased with any payment plan a new member is made active in the ‘Free Membership’ plan in ARMember.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/armember-integration-setting-9c68a9a273.webp)

Once you have finished filling out all the settings needed for your product, complete the order form by selecting Publish or Update.
