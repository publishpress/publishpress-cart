# Creating Product

Products are the items, services, programs, memberships, or digital downloads that you sell with [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart). Each product can have its own price, payment plans, checkout form, order settings, integrations, and post-purchase flow.

To create a product:

1. Go to **Cart > Products**.

2. Click **Add New Product**.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/creating-product-6bceb9335a.webp)

3. Enter the product name in the title field.

4. Add a product description in the main editor.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/enter-title-and-description-cfa00b16e5.webp)

5. Next, go to **Payment Plans** > **Add New**.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/payment-plan-5b33d53e80.webp)

6. Select the **Price Type**, and filled in the **Label**, and **Price**.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/payment-plan-configuration-704bc53fbe.webp)

7. Lastly, **Publish** the product.

You can view the product, and you will have something like this:

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/view-products-2703012abe.webp)

This is the default layout of how product showing in [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart).

There are more additional configuration on the product. You could check this guide.

- [Additional product configuration](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/product-configuration/)
- [Customize product template](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/customize-product-template/)
- Customize product checkout form
