# MailPoet

Table of Contents

- [How to integrate MailPoet with PublishPress Cart](#0-toc-title)

Do you use MailPoet for email marketing? The MailPoet integration allows you to automatically add or remove new users to your email lists. You can integrate your account with [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) by installing the plugin on your WordPress site.

## How to integrate MailPoet with PublishPress Cart [#](#0-toc-title)

Get started by installing and activating the MailPoet plugin on your WordPress site.

From here you can set up a MailPoet integration by going to a [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) product and selecting “MailPoet” from the Service menu in the Integrations tab.

You’ll see a MailPoet List dropdown with a list of all the email lists you previously created in MailPoet. Choose the list you would like to link to this product.

![MailPoet integration settings in a PublishPress Cart product](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/mailpoet-product-integration-75ff538919.webp)

**Note:** If your MailPoet lists are not showing up in the dropdown, click the **Renew mailing lists** button to fetch your latest mailing list info.

In the example above, the following will occur from the settings chosen. When a product is purchased with any payment plan, a contact is added to the chosen mailing list in MailPoet.

You have the option to enable **Require consent**. If turned on, only customers who have given consent will be added to the list. Note, you will also need to turn on the Opt-in Checkbox in the Form Fields tab for this feature to work.

Once you have finished filling out all the settings needed for your product, complete the order form by selecting **Publish** or **Update**.
