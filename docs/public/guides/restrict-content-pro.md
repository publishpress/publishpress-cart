# Restrict Content Pro

Table of Contents

- [How To Integrate Restrict Content Pro With PublishPress Cart](#0-toc-title)

Do you use Restrict Content Pro? The Restrict Content Pro integration allows you to automatically add new members to a membership level. You can integrate your account with [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) by installing the Restrict Content Pro plugin on your WordPress site.

## How To Integrate Restrict Content Pro With PublishPress Cart [#](#0-toc-title)

Get started by installing and activating the Restrict Content Pro plugin on your WordPress site. Then configure the settings in the Restrict Content Pro plugin on your WordPress site, including the Membership Levels you want to use.

![Restrict Content Pro membership levels](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/restrict-content-pro-membership-levels-c2d1b276b1.webp)

Now you can set up a Restrict Content Pro integration by going to a [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) product and selecting “Restrict Content Pro” from the services menu in the Integrations tab.

You’ll see a list of the Membership Levels you previously created in Restrict Content Pro. Choose the Membership Level you would like to link to this product.

In the example below the following will occur from the settings chosen. When a product is purchased with any payment plan a new member is added as ‘Active’ to the ‘Gold’ membership in Restrict Content Pro.

![Restrict Content Pro integration settings in PublishPress Cart](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/restrict-content-pro-integration-settings-2c58fc15a1.webp)

Once you have finished filling out all the settings needed for your product, complete the order form by selecting Publish or Update.
