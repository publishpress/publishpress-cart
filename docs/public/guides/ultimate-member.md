# Ultimate Member

Table of Contents

- [How To Integrate Ultimate Member With PublishPress Cart](#0-toc-title)

Do you use Ultimate Member? The Ultimate Member integration allows you to automatically send a welcome email to your new users. You can integrate your account with [PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart) by installing the plugin on your WordPress site.

## How To Integrate Ultimate Member With PublishPress Cart [#](#0-toc-title)

Get started by installing and activating the Ultimate Member plugin on your WordPress site. You can install it for free from the WordPress plugin directory under **Plugins > Add Plugin**.

![Ultimate Member plugin activated in WordPress](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/ultimate-member-plugin-activated-5e082588d0.webp)

You can add integrations for a single product. Edit the product, open the **[PublishPress Cart](https://publishpress.com/links/cart)** panel and select the **Integrations** tab. Integrations added here apply to that product only..

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-cart/ultimate-member-integration-7a416517f6.webp)

Once the integration is enabled, PublishPress Cart will automatically send the Ultimate Member welcome email to new users created after a purchase.
