# PublishPress Cart branding

You're welcome to use any material on this page when writing about PublishPress
Cart. For company-wide brand materials (company logo, mascots, and shared
guidelines), see the [PublishPress brand page](https://publishpress.com/brand/).

## Description

> PublishPress Cart is a simple, powerful plugin that has everything you need to
> sell digital products, programs, events, or services from your own website —
> exactly the way YOU want to sell them. Create on-brand checkout pages and
> sales flows for your digital products in minutes — no coding necessary.

## Colors

Purple `#655997` appears in Cart admin CSS. Yellow (`#ffb200`) and dark purple
(`#1b004f`) are PublishPress company brand colors documented on the
[PublishPress brand page](https://publishpress.com/brand/) — they are not
necessarily used in the plugin's admin stylesheets.

| Color | HEX | RGB | Notes |
|-------|-----|-----|-------|
| Purple | `#655997` | `rgb(101, 89, 151)` | Cart admin UI |
| Yellow | `#ffb200` | `rgb(255, 178, 0)` | PublishPress company brand |
| Dark purple | `#1b004f` | `rgb(27, 0, 79)` | PublishPress company brand |
