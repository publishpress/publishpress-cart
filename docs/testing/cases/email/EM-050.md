# Subscription Renewal Failed — enabled sends

## Scenario

Automated by `tests/playwright/admin/email-050-failed-enabled-sends.spec.ts`. With **Subscription
Renewal Failed** turned on from the Emails settings tab, a `failed` status change delivers the
customer email.

## Steps

1. Skip unless admin credentials and the email fixture ids (`composer test:admin:setup`) are present.
2. Open `<site>/wp-admin/admin.php?page=ppcart-settings#emails` and confirm `#content_tab_emails`.
3. Open the `emailtemplate_failed` Manage modal.
4. Turn `_ppcart_email_failed_enable` on by clicking `label[for="_ppcart_email_failed_enable"]`; the
   input is `display: none`, so `check()` cannot be used.
5. Save from inside the modal and wait for the `settings-updated=true` redirect.
6. Assert the "Subscription Renewal Failed" row status dot renders `is-enabled`.
7. Empty the Mailpit mailbox.
8. POST the fixtures trigger `order_status` for the seeded **order** with status `failed` and a
   unique `@example.invalid` recipient.

## Expected result

1. A Mailpit message addressed to that recipient arrives within the wait window.
2. Its subject is not empty.

## Edge cases

- Like `renewal`, `failed` builds a `PPCart_Order` rather than a `PPCart_Subscription`, so this fires
  against the order fixture.
- `_ppcart_email_past_due_*` are aliases of the failed options, not an eighth email type.
- The mail reaches `wp_mail()` through `ppcart_run_after_integrations`, which fires unconditionally at
  the end of `ppcart_trigger_integrations()`, rather than through that function's `'failed' &&
  $event_type == 'renewal'` switch arm — that arm compares against a boolean and never matches a
  plain order. A future fix to the switch would therefore not break this case.
