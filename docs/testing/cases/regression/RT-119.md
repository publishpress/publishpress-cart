# Two inline embeds on one page render and size independently

## Scenario

A merchant puts two products on one page. Each frame must show its own product and follow its own height.

Automated by `tests/playwright/regression/rt119-embed-two-inline-embeds-size-independently.spec.ts`. The host page is served from `https://merchant.example` through `page.route`, and its snippet loads `public/js/ppcart-embed.js` from the WordPress site.

The test is skipped unless `url_embed_product_free` and `url_embed_product_stripe` are configured for the target site.

## Steps

1. Use a 1280 x 2000 viewport, so both frames are on screen.
2. Open a host page with inline snippets for the free and the Stripe product and one loader.
3. Tick and untick the terms box in the free product frame.

## Expected result

1. The first frame shows Free access and the second shows One payment of $10.
2. Each iframe is within 4px of its own document height.
3. The free product iframe grows with its terms message, and the Stripe iframe keeps its height.

## Edge cases

- The loader matches each message to its iframe by `event.source` and origin, not by the claimed id.
