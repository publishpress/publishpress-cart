# Over Refunds Are Clamped To Zero Net Amount

## Scenario

If the recorded refund total exceeds the order's gross amount (an over-refund), `PPCart_Order_Refunds::get_net_amount()` must never return a negative value. For a $200.00 order with a stored refund of $250.00, the refund is capped at the gross and the net amount clamps to $0.00.

## Steps

1. Seed order `669` post meta with `_sc_amount = "200.00"` and `_sc_refund_amount = "250.00"` (no refund log).
2. Call `PPCart_Order_Refunds::get_net_amount(669)`.
3. The method parses the gross (200.00), caps the refund total via `min(250.00, 200.00) = 200.00`, then returns `max(0, 200.00 - 200.00)`.

## Expected result

The net amount is exactly `0.0` (float) — never negative — despite the $250.00 refund exceeding the $200.00 gross.

## Edge cases

- A refund total exactly equal to the gross also yields a net of `0.0`.
- The outer `max(0, ...)` guards against any rounding that could otherwise produce a small negative value.
