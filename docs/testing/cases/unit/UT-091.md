# Partial Refund Reduces The Displayed Net Amount

## Scenario

`PPCart_Order_Refunds::get_net_amount()` returns the order's gross amount minus the refund total, representing what the customer effectively paid. For a $200.00 order with a $100.00 partial refund, the net amount should drop to $100.00. This verifies refunds are subtracted from the displayed amount.

## Steps

1. Seed order `667` post meta with `_sc_amount = "200.00"`, `_sc_refund_amount = "100.00"`, and a `_sc_refund_log` entry keyed `re_partial` with `amount = "100.00"`.
2. Call `PPCart_Order_Refunds::get_net_amount(667)`.
3. The method parses the gross amount (200.00), caps the refund total at the gross, and returns `max(0, 200.00 - 100.00)`.

## Expected result

The net amount is exactly `100.0` (float): $200.00 gross minus the $100.00 refund.

## Edge cases

- If the refund total ever exceeded the gross amount, the net would clamp to `0.0` rather than going negative (covered by the over-refund case).
- An empty or unparseable `_sc_amount` yields a gross of `0`, producing a net of `0.0`.
