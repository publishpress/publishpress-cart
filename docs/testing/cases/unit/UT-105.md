# Duplicate Title Is Prefixed With "Copy of"

## Scenario

The copy needs a clearly distinguishable title so admins can tell it apart from the original in list tables. This verifies the duplicate's title is the source title wrapped in the "Copy of %s" template.

## Steps

1. Seed the source product with title `'Configured Product'`.
2. Duplicate it and read the duplicate post object.
3. Inspect its `post_title`.

## Expected result

`post_title` is `'Copy of Configured Product'`, produced by the `__('Copy of %s', ...)` format applied to the source title.

## Edge cases

- The title is derived from the source, not copied verbatim, so identical-named originals remain visually distinguishable.
