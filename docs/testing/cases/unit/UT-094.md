# Legacy Indexed Refund Logs Reduce The Net Amount

## Scenario

When refunds are recorded only in a legacy numerically indexed `_sc_refund_log`, `PPCart_Order_Refunds::get_net_amount()` must still subtract the summed log total from the gross amount. For a $200.00 order with legacy refunds totaling $75.00, the net amount should be $125.00.

## Steps

1. Seed order `668` post meta with `_sc_amount = "200.00"` and an indexed `_sc_refund_log` of two entries: `re_one` at `amount = "25.00"` and `re_two` at `amount = "50.00"`.
2. Call `PPCart_Order_Refunds::get_net_amount(668)`.
3. The method parses the gross (200.00), sums the legacy log to a refund total of 75.00, and returns `max(0, 200.00 - 75.00)`.

## Expected result

The net amount is exactly `125.0` (float): $200.00 gross minus the $75.00 summed legacy refunds.

## Edge cases

- The refund total is capped at the gross amount before subtraction, so a legacy log exceeding the gross cannot drive the net negative.
- A missing `_sc_refund_amount` does not affect the result because the larger of (log sum, stored amount) is used.
