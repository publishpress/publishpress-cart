# Subscription checkout refuses a payment method of another customer

## Scenario

`ppcart_create_subscription` gets the customer from the checkout reference and the
payment method from the bound SetupIntent, or from a posted method that is free or
already on the bound customer.

## Steps

1. Resolve a payment method with a SetupIntent that belongs to another customer.
2. Resolve with a good SetupIntent while posting another method; then with a SetupIntent that did not succeed or came from another site.
3. Without a SetupIntent, post a method attached to another customer, then a free method, then a customer ID.

## Expected result

1. Refused.
2. The SetupIntent method is used, not the posted one. Unfinished or foreign SetupIntents are refused.
3. The foreign method is refused and not attached. The free method is attached to the bound customer. The customer ID is refused.

## Edge cases

- The request is refused before any customer update when the reference is missing.
