# Importing a Stripe subscription creates it with its orders

## Scenario

A Stripe subscription made before StudioCart or Cart has no Cart record and no Cart metadata. The admin imports it from Cart > Settings > Maintenance > Import from Stripe.

## Steps

1. Create a product that sells the subscription's Stripe price.
2. Stripe has the subscription, canceled, with two paid invoices and no metadata.
3. Import the subscription by its sub_ ID.

## Expected result

Cart creates the subscription with the Stripe ID, status, product, customer email and Stripe customer. Both invoices become orders with the customer's email, and the first one is the subscription's first order.
