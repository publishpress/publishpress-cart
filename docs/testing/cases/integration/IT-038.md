# Refund log normalizes mixed-shape entries to be keyed by refund id

## Scenario

Refund log normalization must accept refund logs that mix two shapes: entries that carry their refund identifier inside the entry, and entries that are already filed under their refund identifier. After normalization, every entry must be addressable by its refund identifier so downstream deduplication and display can rely on a single, consistent structure.

## Steps

1. Start with a refund log containing one entry that carries its refund identifier inside it (not yet filed under that identifier) and one entry already filed under its refund identifier.
2. Run the refund log through normalization.

## Expected result

The normalized refund log is filed by refund identifier and contains both refunds: the first promoted from inside the entry, and the second preserved from its existing place.

## Edge cases

- Entries that are already filed under their refund identifier must be preserved as-is rather than re-filed or dropped.
