# PayPal cart checkout

## Scenario

PayPal on the page pays a cart order through one PayPal order. The site
believes the answer that it reads from PayPal, and never a status that the
browser sends.

## Steps

1. Submit a cart with two items and PayPal.
2. Confirm the order before the buyer approves it. Confirm it when the PayPal
   order names another local order, another currency, or another total, and
   when the capture has another amount.
3. Approve the order at PayPal and confirm it two times.
4. Call the on-page approval with a wrong token. Call it with the order token
   and a completed status from the browser before PayPal approves the order,
   and again after PayPal approves it.

## Expected result

One pending order without product meta is stored. One PayPal purchase unit is
created for the stored total, and its description is the order name. The cancel
URL is the checkout page from the Cart settings. The return URL is that page
with the order ID and its access value. The approved order that matches is
captured and becomes paid. After the two confirmations there is one purchase
email, a download grant for both products, one stock decrease for each product,
and no paid line in the cart.

## Edge cases

- A browser claim of completion cannot pay the order.
- A PayPal answer with another order, currency, total, or capture amount leaves
  the order pending. No email goes out and the cart keeps its lines.
- The on-page approval refuses a wrong token. Before PayPal approves the order,
  it asks the browser to try again and the order stays pending.
