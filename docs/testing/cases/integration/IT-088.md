# Account Subscriptions Block Lists Current Customer Subscriptions

## Scenario

The Account Subscriptions block must list the logged-in customer's active subscriptions under an active-subscriptions heading.

## Steps

1. As a logged-in customer who has one subscription, render the Account Subscriptions block.

## Expected result

- The Account Subscriptions block renders an active-subscriptions heading and lists the customer's subscription beneath it.

## Edge cases

- Skipped when a temporary account customer (and their subscription) cannot be created.
