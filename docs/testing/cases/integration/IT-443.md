# Shop visibility and offers

## Scenario

The Shop lists only the products that a merchant shows there. Each card offers
only the plans that a cart can hold. A product that is hidden from the Shop must
still sell through Buy now.

## Steps

1. Save a product with Show in Shop Page unchecked. Render the Shop and the
   Buy now checkout of that product.
2. Check the field and save. Save again without the General submission marker.
3. Render a product with two one-time plans and one subscription plan. One plan
   ID contains a colon.
4. Read the card of a subscription-only product. Turn the shopping cart off and
   read the card of the first product.
5. Run the Shop query with a filter that asks for any status, another post type,
   and no meta query.
6. Set the stock of a product to zero. Then give it a sales window that starts
   in the future. Then give it a free plan that has a URL alias.
7. Query the Shop in price order with one product on each page.
8. Resolve the shared style tokens for empty settings, unsafe values, the
   minimal preset, and a density-only setting.
9. Render the Shop with subscription products excluded, one product on each
   page. The products are one-time, mixed, and subscription-only.
10. Read the cards of a product with two named one-time plans, a mixed product,
    a product with two subscription plans, and one-time plans with no price or
    a zero price.
11. Render the Shop with each pagination style on page 1 and on the last page,
    and with an unknown style.
12. Render the Shop with saved surface, density, and corner settings, and with
    an accent color or a preset.

## Expected result

The unchecked setting stays `no`. The Shop omits the product and Buy now still
renders. The checked field stores `yes`, and a save without the marker keeps
that value. The selector holds only the two one-time plans, selects the first,
and keeps the colon in the plan ID. The subscription-only card has the Subscribe
action. With the cart off, the card has the View action. The filtered query
still returns only published and visible products. Zero stock and a future sales
window make the product not eligible. The free plan has the price 0 and the
label Free. The cheaper product is on page 1 and the other product is on page 2.
Empty settings give no tokens, and the minimal preset gives its native values.
Without subscriptions, the subscription-only product is gone, the mixed product
stays, and the pages count only the products that remain. A card price is the
price of the plan that Add to cart adds, and each option carries its plan name
and price. Several subscription plans still show From. A one-time plan with no
price or a zero price shows Free. Page numbers, Previous and next, and Load more
render their own controls, the last page has no next link or button, Show all
renders every product with no controls, and an unknown style falls back to page
numbers. Saved surface, density, and corner settings add no tokens, while the
accent color and a preset still apply.

## Edge cases

- A subscription plan and an unknown plan are refused with
  `ppcart_cart_plan_not_eligible`.
- A plan alias is refused. Only the plan ID identifies a plan.
- The removed `[ppcart_store]` shortcode stays as literal text. The Shop block
  and `[ppcart_shop]` render the same catalog.
- Unsafe style values give no tokens. A density-only setting sets no accent.
