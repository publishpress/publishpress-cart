# Cart checkout gate: purchase limits and stock

Give a product a purchase limit of one and a paid order for one customer. Put
the product in a cart and claim the checkout with that customer's email. The
claim is refused with the product's own limit message, the cart keeps its
revision, and no claim is written. Another customer claims the same cart.

A paid cart order counts toward the limit through its main item. A bump item of
a Buy now order does not count. A signed-in customer is matched by account when
the email differs.

Put two plans of that product in one cart. The lines count together with the
paid orders, so a limit of one refuses the claim on the second line and tells
the buyer how many purchases are left. A paid cart order with two lines of the
product counts as two purchases.

Put two plans of one product in a cart with a stock of one. The claim is refused
because the lines need two. With a stock of two the claim succeeds. The sales
window still closes a product whose stock is zero.
