# A charged invoice after a $0 invoice gets its own order

## Scenario

A subscription's first invoice was $0 (trial or coupon) and synced onto its first order. The next invoice is charged.

## Expected result

The charged invoice becomes a new order. The $0 order keeps its invoice ID and gets no charge ID.
