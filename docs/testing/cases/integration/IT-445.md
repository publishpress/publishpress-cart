# Cart checkout order and payment

## Scenario

The cart checkout places one order and one Stripe PaymentIntent for the cart
that the buyer saw. It trusts no amount and no product from the browser.

## Steps

1. Submit the cart checkout with two products and Stripe. Put a product ID and
   an amount in the request.
2. Confirm the order before Stripe reports the payment, with a payment that
   names another order, and with a wrong token. Confirm the succeeded payment
   two times. Confirm a Buy now order.
3. Change a price after the form loaded and submit. Submit the old revision
   again. Submit the new revision two times.
4. Submit with missing fields, with a pay method that is not available, with a
   product at its purchase limit, and with a gateway error.
5. Submit a free cart. Turn on cash on delivery. Submit a paid cart without a
   pay method, then with cash on delivery.
6. Call the checkout route without a REST nonce, with an empty body, and with
   the cart turned off. Call the confirm route without its arguments.

## Expected result

One pending order is stored with both items. One PaymentIntent is created for
the order total, with the cart metadata, the order description, the Stripe
Connect fee, and an idempotency key for the order. The payment data has a
return address on the checkout page. The order becomes paid only when Stripe
reports a succeeded payment that names that order. The completion work runs one
time and the cart is empty. After the price change the request is refused with
the new cart, and no order or payment exists. The next submit with the new
revision pays the new total.

## Edge cases

- A product ID or an amount in the request changes nothing.
- A wrong token, the payment of another order, and a Buy now order are refused.
  A second confirmation does not run the completion work again.
- A second submit of the same form is refused.
- Missing fields, a pay method that is not available, and a purchase limit are
  refused before the cart is claimed. The cart keeps its revision.
- A gateway error leaves a pending order without a payment. The cart keeps its
  lines.
- A free cart is paid without a gateway. Cash on delivery stores a pending
  order and empties the cart.
- Both routes need a REST nonce and an enabled cart. With the cart off, the
  form renders nothing.
