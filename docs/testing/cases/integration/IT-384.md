# Account subscription detail shows only the customer's own subscription

## Scenario

`[ppcart_account_subscription_detail]` takes a subscription ID from the
request or the `plan` attribute. Another customer must not see the
subscription, its gateway subscription ID, or its cancel / pause controls.

## Steps

1. Customer A owns an active subscription.
2. Log in as customer B and render the shortcode for A's subscription.
3. Log out and render it again.
4. Log in as customer A and render it again.
5. As customer B, render the `my-account/subscription-detail` template directly.

## Expected result

- Customer B and the guest get no subscription data and no cancel control.
- Customer A sees the subscription.
- The template itself shows "No subscription found." to customer B.
