# Account Orders Block Renders Order History And Fires Extension Hook

## Scenario

The Account Orders block must list the current customer's orders and offer an extension point on the orders tab so add-ons can inject extra content there.

## Steps

1. As a logged-in customer who has one order, register an add-on that adds extra content to the orders tab.
2. Render the Account Orders block.

## Expected result

- The Account Orders block renders the customer's order history, including their order.
- The extra content contributed by the add-on through the orders-tab extension point appears within the block.

## Edge cases

- Skipped when a temporary account customer (and their order) cannot be created.
