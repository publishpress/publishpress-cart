# PayPal cart checkout

A two-item cart creates one pending order without product meta and one PayPal
purchase unit for its stored total. The description is the order name. PayPal
return and cancel URLs use the Cart settings.

A browser claim of completion cannot pay the order. PayPal responses with a
different order, currency, total, or capture amount leave it pending. A matching
approved order is captured and becomes paid. Repeated confirmation sends one
purchase email, grants both downloads, decreases each product's stock once,
and removes the paid cart lines.
