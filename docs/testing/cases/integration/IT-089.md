# Account Payment Plans Block Lists Installment Subscriptions

## Scenario

The Account Payment Plans block must show the customer's installment-based payment plans (fixed-term plans) under an active-plans heading, separate from open-ended subscriptions.

## Steps

1. As a logged-in customer who has one fixed-term installment payment plan, render the Account Payment Plans block.

## Expected result

- The Account Payment Plans block renders an active-plans heading and lists the customer's payment plan beneath it.

## Edge cases

- Skipped when a temporary account customer (and their payment plan) cannot be created.
