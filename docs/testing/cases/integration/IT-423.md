# Invoice sync does not restore the next payment of a pending cancellation

## Scenario

A subscription is set to cancel in Stripe. The subscription sync clears its next payment date. "Sync with Stripe" then imports the subscription's invoices. The newest invoice's period end is the day the subscription ends, not a billing date, so it must not become the next payment date again.

## Steps

1. Start with an active Stripe subscription that is pending cancellation: `cancel_at` and `cancel_date` set, no next payment date.
2. Sync a paid invoice of that subscription whose line period ends on the cancellation date.
3. Check the stored next payment date.

## Expected result

The next payment date stays empty.

## Edge cases

- A subscription that isn't pending cancellation still takes its next payment date from the invoice period end.
