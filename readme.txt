== Description ==

**PublishPress Cart turns your WordPress site into a complete store for digital products, online courses, coaching, memberships, events, and services.**

PublishPress Cart makes selling digital products with WordPress simple. Everything you need to start selling is in the main Cart plugin: payment plans, subscriptions, secure file delivery, customer accounts, and automations, with no add-ons to install.

PublishPress Cart gives you a fast, flexible checkout plus more than 50 integrations for email marketing, courses, memberships, automation, analytics, affiliates, and much more.

## What you can sell ##

* **Digital downloads** such as ebooks, templates, workbooks, software, and audio files.
* **Online courses and programs** with customers sent to your course platform automatically.
* **Coaching and consulting packages** paid in full, in installments, or as a monthly retainer.
* **Memberships and subscriptions** with free trials, sign-up fees, and recurring billing.
* **Events, workshops, and services** with sales limits for capped seats.

## Checkout pages that match your site ##

A PublishPress Cart checkout is a regular part of your WordPress site, so it uses your theme, your fonts, and your design.

* **Checkout block.** Add the Checkout block to any page or post. Choose the product, set the default payment plan, rename the form's headings and labels, and set wide or full-width alignment.
* **Works with the Site Editor.** You can use the WordPress Site Editor to design checkout pages, collections/bundles, and product pages.
* **Shortcodes and page builders.** Use a shortcode anywhere, or build checkouts with native widgets in Elementor, Divi, and Oxygen.
* **Two Stripe checkout styles.** Keep buyers on your site with Stripe's embedded payment form, or send them to Stripe-hosted checkout to offer methods such as Klarna and Cash App Pay.
* **Marketing consent.** Add an optional or required opt-in checkbox with your own wording.
* **Spam protection.** Protect every checkout form with Google reCAPTCHA.

## Flexible pricing and payment plans ##

Give each product as many payment plans as you need, and let buyers choose the option that works for them.

* **One-time payments** for straightforward purchases.
* **Installment plans** that charge a set number of payments and then stop.
* **Recurring subscriptions** billed daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly until canceled.
* **Free trials** of any number of days before the first subscription payment.
* **Sign-up fees** charged up front on recurring plans.
* **Pay-what-you-want pricing** that lets buyers set their own amount.
* **Free products** for lead magnets and opt-in offers.
* **Sales.** Put a product on sale now, or schedule a sale with start and end dates, and show the original price next to the sale price.
* **Hidden plans.** Keep a plan off your checkout forms without deleting it.

## Product launches and limited offers ##

* **Scheduled cart opening.** Open and close a checkout on set dates, or switch it on and off by hand.
* **Sales limits.** Close a product automatically after a set number of sales, and limit how many times each customer can buy.
* **Closed-cart redirects.** Send visitors to a waitlist or sales page when the cart is closed.
* **Stock alerts.** Get an email when a limited product is running low or sold out.

## Delivery after purchase ##

* **Thank-you pages.** Send buyers to a custom thank-you page or to any URL.
* **Secure file delivery.** Attach files to a product, limit the number of downloads, restrict files to specific payment plans, and choose whether to force a download or redirect to the file.
* **Customer account area.** Build a "My Account" page with blocks for orders, subscriptions, payment plans, downloads, profile, and login.
* **Customizable emails.** Edit the order received, purchase confirmation, welcome, renewal, and cancellation emails, using personalization tags for names, order details, and links. Add extra notifications per product, for example to alert a coach when a new client books.

## Orders, subscriptions, and customers ##

* **Orders.** See every order with its customer, products, amount, and status. Open an order to edit billing details, resend the confirmation email, or download an invoice or receipt as a PDF.
* **Refunds.** Issue full or partial refunds from the order screen.
* **Subscriptions.** Pause, resume, or cancel any subscription, either immediately or at the end of the billing period, with or without a refund.
* **Customers.** View each buyer's order history and lifetime value, and export your customer list.
* **Reports.** Track revenue and sales in WordPress, and have sales reports emailed to you on a schedule.

## Automations and integrations ##

Each product can run its own automations. Choose what triggers them, such as a purchase, a refund, a new subscription, a renewal, a failed payment, a pause, or a cancellation, and limit them to specific payment plans.

* **Email marketing.** Add or tag buyers in ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, or SendFox.
* **Courses.** Give buyers access to MemberVault products.
* **WordPress user accounts.** Create a WordPress user with the role you choose when someone buys.
* **WooCommerce.** Keep PublishPress Cart's checkout and copy each order into WooCommerce, matched to your WooCommerce products and set to the order status you choose.
* **Order actions.** Automatically refund another order or cancel another subscription, which is useful for upgrades and replacements.
* **Webhooks.** Send order details to any URL with a GET or POST request, so you can connect almost any other service.
* **Tracking.** Send purchase and lead events to Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, and add your own tracking code to each product.

[Read how integrations work](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/integrations-overview/).

## External services ##

PublishPress Cart can connect to third-party services when you enable and configure the related feature. These connections are optional unless you choose the related payment gateway, spam-protection feature, analytics feature, or marketing integration.

= Meta Pixel =

If you enter a Meta Pixel ID in the Facebook Ad Events integration, PublishPress Cart loads Meta Pixel from https://connect.facebook.net on checkout and paid order confirmation pages. Meta receives PageView events and any PublishPress Cart events you enable, which can include the product ID, price, currency, and an order event ID. Meta Pixel may collect browser and device data and use cookies. A Meta account and a Pixel ID are required for this optional setup. If your site already loads Meta Pixel, PublishPress Cart can send enabled events to that installation.

Service: Meta Platforms, Inc. Terms: [https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms](https://www.facebook.com/legal/technology_terms). Privacy: [https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

= Stripe =

Stripe is used to process card payments when Stripe is enabled. During checkout, PublishPress Cart sends order, customer, amount, currency, and payment metadata needed to create and confirm Stripe payments. Stripe may return payment, charge, refund, subscription, and webhook event data to the site. When a Stripe publishable key is saved, checkout and product pages also load [Stripe.js](https://js.stripe.com/v3/).

Service: Stripe, Inc. Terms: [https://stripe.com/legal](https://stripe.com/legal). Privacy: [https://stripe.com/privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

= Stripe Connect (PublishPress) =

The optional Stripe Connect button uses a PublishPress connection service at https://publishpress.com to complete Stripe OAuth. When you start Connect from plugin settings, the plugin sends the site URL, return URL, connection state, and an encrypted site credential needed to finish account linking. PublishPress returns Stripe account identifiers used to process payments. This happens only when you choose Connect Stripe.

When Stripe Connect is used without PublishPress Cart Pro, PublishPress charges a 2% application fee on those connected payments, collected through Stripe. That fee is not charged when PublishPress Cart Pro is active.

Service: PublishPress LLC. Terms: [https://publishpress.com/terms-of-service/](https://publishpress.com/terms-of-service/). Privacy: [https://publishpress.com/privacy-policy/](https://publishpress.com/privacy-policy/).

= PayPal =

PayPal processes payments and subscriptions when PayPal is enabled. During checkout and subscription management, PublishPress Cart sends order, customer, amount, currency, subscription, and transaction data needed to create, verify, refund, pause, resume, or cancel PayPal transactions.

Service: PayPal, Inc. Terms: [https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/useragreement-full](https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/useragreement-full). Privacy: [https://www.paypal.com/privacy](https://www.paypal.com/privacy).

= Google reCAPTCHA =

Google reCAPTCHA is used to help validate checkout form submissions when reCAPTCHA is enabled. PublishPress Cart sends the reCAPTCHA response token and the visitor IP address to Google for verification. When a reCAPTCHA site key is saved, checkout forms also load [https://www.google.com/recaptcha/api.js](https://www.google.com/recaptcha/api.js) from Google.

Service: Google LLC. Terms: [https://policies.google.com/terms](https://policies.google.com/terms). Privacy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

= SendFox =

SendFox is used to add buyers to configured mailing lists when the SendFox integration is enabled. PublishPress Cart sends subscriber and order-related data required by the configured integration, such as email address, name, and selected list information.

Service: SendFox / Sumo Group, Inc. Terms: [https://sendfox.com/tos](https://sendfox.com/tos). Privacy: [https://sendfox.com/privacy](https://sendfox.com/privacy).

== Installation ==

1. Go to Plugins > Add New in your WordPress admin and search for "PublishPress Cart", or upload the plugin files to `/wp-content/plugins/`.
2. Activate the plugin.
3. Open the **PublishPress Cart** menu in your WordPress admin to connect a payment gateway and create your first product.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Who is PublishPress Cart for? =

Course creators, coaches, consultants, membership sites, event organizers, and anyone selling digital products or services from WordPress. If you sell physical products that need shipping and a catalog, a plugin like WooCommerce may suit you better.

= Do I need WooCommerce? =

No. PublishPress Cart is a standalone checkout and does not need WooCommerce or any other eCommerce plugin. If you already run WooCommerce, you can still connect it with the WooCommerce integration.

= Which payment processors can I use? =

Stripe and PayPal. With Stripe you can use the embedded card form or Stripe-hosted checkout, which supports extra methods such as Klarna and Cash App Pay. [PublishPress Cart Pro](https://publishpress.com/cart/) adds Mollie, Razorpay, and Square.

= Can I sell subscriptions and payment plans? =

Yes. Each product can have several plans: one-time payments, installment plans, recurring subscriptions, free trials, sign-up fees, pay what you want, and free.

= Can customers manage their own subscriptions? =

Yes. Add the account page blocks to a page and customers can see their orders, downloads, subscriptions, and payment plans in one place.

= Which integrations are available for PublishPress Cart? =

Academy LMS, ActiveCampaign, Affiliates Add-on, Albato, ARMember, AutomatorWP, Bit Integrations, Circle, Divi, Drip, Elementor, Encharge, FluentCRM, GamiPress, Google Analytics, Groups, Gurucan, Heartbeat, Integrately, Kajabi, Kit (formerly ConvertKit), Knit, KonnectzIT, LearnDash, Mailchimp, MailerLite, MasterStudy LMS, MemberPress, MemberVault, Meta Pixel, OttoKit (formerly SureTriggers), Oxygen, Pabbly Connect, PublishPress Future, PublishPress Permissions, Razorpay Payment Gateway, Restrict Content Pro, SegMetrics, SendFox, SliceWP, Teachable, Thrive Automator, Tutor LMS, Ultimate Member, Uncanny Automator, Webhooks, WishList Member, WooCommerce, WP Courseware, WP Fusion, WP Webhooks Automator, and Zapier.

Available integrations vary by plan. See the [Free vs Pro comparison](https://publishpress.com/knowledge-base/difference-between-cart-free-and-pro/).

= How does PublishPress Cart compare to other eCommerce options? =

There are many options, including WooCommerce, Easy Digital Downloads, SureCart, Ecwid, BigCommerce for WordPress, WP EasyCart, FluentCart, MemberPress, Paid Memberships Pro, Restrict Content Pro, ARMember, WishList Member, WP Simple Pay, GiveWP, Charitable, Dokan, WC Vendors, CartFlows, and FunnelKit.

We're different in three key ways:

* **Built for digital products and services.** PublishPress Cart is focused on memberships, courses, file downloads, bookings, and online offers. It does not handle shipping for physical products.
* **Core features in one plugin.** Payment plans, subscriptions, file delivery, customer accounts, and automations are built in rather than split across dozens of add-ons.
* **Connected to the PublishPress suite.** PublishPress Cart works alongside other PublishPress plugins. For example, you can generate QR codes or short links with PublishPress Shortlinks, schedule access changes with PublishPress Future, or manage access with PublishPress Permissions.

PublishPress Cart isn't trying to out-muscle WooCommerce for massive, complex retail catalogs. Instead, it's designed to be simpler, faster to set up, and budget-friendly. It starts at $79/year for a single site, giving most digital creators and site owners what they need in one place.

= Can I use my page builder? =

Yes. Use the Checkout block in the block editor, a shortcode in any builder, or the Elementor, Divi, and Oxygen integrations.

= Is PublishPress Cart related to Studiocart? =

Yes. PublishPress Cart is the actively maintained successor to Studiocart, and is based on Studiocart 2.9.0. Studiocart has closed. [Contact us](https://publishpress.com/publishpress-support/) for a migration path.

= Is there a fee on Stripe payments? =

When you connect Stripe with the optional Connect Stripe button and PublishPress Cart Pro is not active, PublishPress charges a 2% application fee on those payments, collected through Stripe. There is no fee when Pro is active.
